Faults and verification

  • Parser: the shared parser read every date month first. For tenants whose locale writes day first, ambiguous dates swapped day and month. Engineering reports a fix. Verify both day-first and month-first tenants and the app, email and booking-v2 form paths.
  • Hint: booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY, while its form interpreted entries according to a day-first tenant’s locale. People following the displayed hint supplied a value the form misread. Engineering retired that form and will not fix it. Confirm that the retired path can no longer take bookings; any replacement must make its hint, example, validation and parser agree.

A fixed parser and a retired form are different outcomes. The timing observations still need checking before assuming that every symptom was addressed. Intake’s browser-derived tag is a guess; it does not replace the tenant directory. Unknown tenants stay unplaced for investigation.
