0002 · Propose duplicate checks by cause

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Context

The subject-only rule in accepted decision 0001 merges different faults and can separate differently worded reports of one fault. The review of #4471 shows why one merged ticket is not evidence of one fix.

Proposed decision

Read the report and consult the tenant’s locale and booking form. Use channel and supported cause evidence to identify duplicates. Subject matches can find candidates, but cannot decide a merge. Unknown tenants or unexplained observations stay separate pending investigation.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading. Each engineering ticket has a supported scope and an independently computed count. The desk must approve this proposal before replacing its operational policy. Decision 0001 remains intact as history.
